## About
Welcome to Papatsiri coffee-shop README! This document provides an overview of our cozy coffee shop, its mission, offerings, and how to get in touch with us.

At Papatsiri coffee-shop, our mission is to provide the highest quality coffee and a warm, inviting atmosphere where people can gather, connect, and enjoy moments of respite from their busy lives.
